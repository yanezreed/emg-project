Dispatch:

- Orders are normally dispatched within 1 to 2 business days
- Orders are dispatched monday to friday

Returns and refunds:

- Returns are accepted within 14 calendar days of delivery
- Returned items must be unopened and in resellable condition
- Customers pay return postage unless the return is caused by a business error
- Refunds are processed within 3 to 5 business days after the returned item is received
- Original postage costs are non refundable unless the business made an error

Defects:

- Photographs and a description of the defect are required
